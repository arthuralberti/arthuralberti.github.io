I am an economist based in São Paulo, Brazil, and hold a Master's degree in Economic Theory from the University of São Paulo. Alongside my studies, I work as a Research Assistant for [Claudio Ferraz](https://sites.google.com/site/claudferraz/home) and [Caroline Le Pennec](https://sites.google.com/berkeley.edu/clpennec) on *Promises, Policies, and Accountability*.

My research interests lie at the intersection of political economy, public policy, and development economics, with a focus on how local institutions and political incentives shape policy implementation. I received my bachelor's degree in economics from Insper and studied as a visiting undergraduate student at the University of California, Berkeley.
